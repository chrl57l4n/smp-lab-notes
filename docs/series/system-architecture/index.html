A new chat and a hello are enough: the wake-up layer at the bottom of the system

A new session starts empty. Before the first answer, two files have to arrive: a small hand-kept map that is always loaded, and a gateway that is built fresh at every start. This note follows a single greeting through that sequence, explains why there are two files and not one, and says how both are kept from growing past the point where they stop being loaded.

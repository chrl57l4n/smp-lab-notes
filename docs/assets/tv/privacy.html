Motoko TV — Privacy Policy

Motoko is a private, non-commercial app for a single household television. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the read-only scope youtube.readonly, the app reads the list of YouTube channels the signed-in account is subscribed to and the latest public videos of those channels, in order to show them in a row on the household TV.

What it does with the data

  • The data is processed only on the household's own server and shown on the household's own TV.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app never writes to, changes, or deletes anything in the YouTube account.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms

The app is provided as is, for private use within one household only.
